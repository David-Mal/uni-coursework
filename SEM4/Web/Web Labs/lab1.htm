About Messi

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He is widely regarded as the greatest footballer ever to play the game. Currently playing for Inter Miami in MLS, Messi has had an extraordinary career spanning over two decades.

Messi spent the majority of his career at FC Barcelona, where he won countless titles and broke numerous records before moving to PSG and then Inter Miami.
